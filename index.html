A Lot To Say About Nothing

Early July, 2026

                The house is burning,
                I think I'll have a drink.

                The house is burning,
                So I sat, and spoke to closed eyes.

                How many times can we stay the same,
                    before we are covered in dust?

                How many steps can we take,
                    before we are walking on ghosts? 

                Each life,
                        is a stone,
                            cast to the wind.

                Just as a mountain soars above your sight,

                Just as the sand between your toes,

                Each life,
                        is a stone,
                            cast to the wind.

                As a mountain falls to the sea,
                    and the sand becomes the breeze,

                Each life,
                        is a stone,
                            cast to the wind.

                As a star turns rock to heat,
                            and we return to dust,
                                        we reverberate,
                
                like the ripples in a pond,
                            after I drop a stone
                into the emptiness
                                beneath
                                      the
                                        surface.
                
                I walk in circles, to see what we really are,

                To see the motion of all things,
                    as
                      they
                         are.

                And in my madness,
                    I choked on ash,
                        I fell to my knees,
                
                The house is still burning,

                and I am consumed by heat.
